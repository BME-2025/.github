# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming and respectful space for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Give constructive feedback focused on ideas and work, not people.
- Welcome questions, differing viewpoints, and good-faith corrections.
- Help keep discussions relevant and accessible to participants.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable. Deliberately disruptive behavior is also not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not post reports publicly. Maintainers will review reports fairly, keep them as confidential as practical, and may remove content or restrict participation when needed. Maintainers are expected to follow these same standards.
